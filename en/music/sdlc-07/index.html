Dar

3:57+

About the track

Dar is a song I created around the B major and B minor scales. Moving between them creates captivating melodic lines and a sense of emotional tension. The song represents giving yourself fully to a mission.

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Ethereal, Warm, Reflective, Peaceful

Genre and musical style

New Age / meditative, Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Neoclassical / piano led

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, cinematic sounds, synths

Scene or visual suggestion

The scene captures the Annunciation at the moment the Angel Gabriel reveals the Virgin Mary’s mission. Fear comes first, followed by humility, wonder, and a growing sense of hope. She feels overwhelmed that she has been chosen, yet she recognizes her strength and accepts what lies ahead. Her emotions move through the uncertainty of “Yes. Yes! Yes? Wait... I said yes, YES.” holding emotions at the same time.

romA

4:18+

About the track

romA is written backward, which is why the capital letter appears at the end. When reversed, it becomes Amor, the Spanish word for love. The track captures a moment created with love, with birds chirping live, life unfolding in the background, and my piano flowing alongside it. I love you.

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Ethereal, Warm, Reflective, Peaceful

Genre and musical style

New Age / meditative, Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Neoclassical / piano led

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, live recorded sounds

Scene or visual suggestion

A room with large windows overlooking trees and palm trees, with birds flying across a blue Nevada sky filled with cinematic clouds. A piano sits near the window while cars pass below, a bus stops, and everyday life continues. The scene feels open and alive, as though the song itself is breathing with the world outside.

03

OOO2

2:50+

About the track

I spent an entire day writing OOO2, completely dedicated to it. There was no food and no distractions, only the song and its flow. Inspired by the sinking boat scene in Life of Pi, the track feels both tense and weightless. You move through it as if struggling against a persistent droning current.

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Ethereal

Genre and musical style

Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Neoclassical / piano led

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, synths, tubas

Scene or visual suggestion

A ship moves through the night toward an unfamiliar destination. Inside a small cabin, anticipation is mixed with unease. Then the vessel begins to sink. Panic spreads through the corridors as alarms and Morse code distress signals cut through the darkness. Everything tilts, shifts, and falls. The deep blue ocean becomes now this black liquid before your eyes, demanding your body, your blood, and your soul as the struggle against drowning takes over.
