01

que Soñaba en el piano

3:00+

About the track

I wrote this song while sitting at the piano after receiving unexpected criticism from someone close to me about a musical collaboration. I was trying to process the response and the doubt it created. Was my judgment that far off? Could I no longer distinguish between something good and something sloppy? Was I becoming sloppy myself? The experience deeply unsettled me, and the song emerged from that confusion.

Mood and atmosphere

Cinematic, Dreamlike, Reflective, Peaceful

Genre and musical style

New Age / meditative, Singer songwriter

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, voice

Scene or visual suggestion

A quiet room after a difficult conversation. The piano remains still while the same words replay in the mind, creating a pause filled with questions. Nothing outwardly changes, but the familiar space feels different as confidence quietly gives way to uncertainty.

About the track

You want a tense song? David Jones Snow is your song. It answers the doubts raised by the previous track. Forget all of that. This is my world, and I create the sounds I want. 03

Dream 2

3:15+

About the track

This song builds on the foundation of another song that is dear to me, using its powerful synths to create something energetic and fun. My sister felt inspired by the music and sent me a set of English lyrics. I selected the lines that fit the song, edited them, and incorporated some of them into the final version. I sing them with a strong accent, since Spanish is my first language, and I embrace that playfulness throughout the track. At one point, I even say, “I don’t even know what I said, for real.”

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Tense, Dynamic

Genre and musical style

Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Neoclassical / piano led, Singer songwriter

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, synths, voices

Scene or visual suggestion

A woman sits motionless in the center of a room while everything around her continues to move. She feels defeated, and even as she travels from place to place, the same pain follows and weighs on her. Life begins to feel like sleepwalking through a dream until she can no longer tell whether she is awake, asleep, or simply going through the motions. Exhausted, she wants only to return to sleep and dream again.

06

Wait for me UNO

3:42+

About the track

In music, counting is very important, especially finding your way back to the first beat so everything stays consistent. I sometimes struggle with that, so this song represents my constant chase of the one. That is why it is called “Wait for Me, Uno.” I am calling out, “Wait for me! Uno, wait!” The song turns that musical chase into its own story.

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Ethereal, Warm, Peaceful

Genre and musical style

New Age / meditative, Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Neoclassical / piano led

Instrumentation and sound

Paino, synths

Scene or visual suggestion

Numbers move in a repeating circle while the number one keeps slipping just out of reach. Each time the rhythm seems ready to settle, the pattern shifts and the chase begins again. The scene feels playful rather than desperate, like trying to catch the first beat before the music moves forward without you.
