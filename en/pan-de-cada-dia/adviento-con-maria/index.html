First Sunday Trust

Mary faces the unknown: The Annunciation

Gospel Luke 1:26–38
Read the full passage on the Vatican website

For reflection

  1. If you were there, what would you ask or say to Mary or someone else in the scene?
  2. What is one thing you’re unsure about right now? What could you say to God as you place it in His hands?

Can I trust God even when I don’t understand what He is doing in my life?

Second Sunday Charity

Mary Visits Her Relative, Saint Elizabeth

Gospel Luke 1:39–45
Read the full passage on the Vatican website
A moment with Mary

It is a deeply human scene: a pregnant woman sets out on a journey to be close to another pregnant woman. Mary has reason to focus on her own situation. Yet she goes to visit her relative. The Gospel does not tell us how much she suffered on the journey, but it does show us that she did not turn inward.

For reflection

  1. Imagine you’re there when Mary greets Elizabeth. What would you ask or say to either of them?
  2. Who might need your company this week? How could you be there for them, even with worries of your own?

Am I so caught up in my own difficulties that I’ve stopped seeing what others need?

Third Sunday Joy

Mary discovers the joy of the promise

Gospel Luke 1:46–55
Read the full passage on the Vatican website
A moment with Mary

Mary’s joy does not depend on everything in her life being easy. It comes from recognizing what God is doing.

For reflection

  1. Imagine hearing Mary praise God. What would you ask or say to her as you listen?
  2. What are three things God is doing in you that you can thank Him for, even during a difficult week?

Can I recognize God’s presence in the midst of my worries?

Fourth Sunday Trustful surrender

Mary draws closer to giving birth

Gospel Luke 2:1–7
Read the full passage on the Vatican website
A moment with Mary

As birth nears, a mother feels vulnerable. She needs protection, worries about her baby and wants a good place to give birth. Mary and Joseph found no room and laid the Christ Child in a manger.

For reflection

  1. Imagine you’re beside Mary or Joseph in the passage you chose. What would you ask or say?
  2. Think of something that didn’t go as you hoped. What is one way you could respond with patience and trust God today?

Am I willing to welcome Christ even when his will does not align with my plans?

But what is an act of trust in God’s providence?

An act of trust means choosing to believe that God is with us, even when things don’t go as planned or we don’t know what will happen next.

Making the Sign of the Cross is an outward gesture. It becomes an act of trust when we do it with faith, consciously placing our worries in God’s hands.

Five ways to live that trust

  1. Before something difficult

    Make the Sign of the Cross. Pray, “Lord, I trust in you. Be with me.”

  2. When you have a decision to make

    Ask God for wisdom to do what is right. Pray, “God, it’s in your hands.”

  3. When a worry keeps coming back

    Once you’ve done what you can, entrust the worry to God.

  4. When your plans change

    Pause before you complain. Ask God to help you respond with patience.

  5. When you feel afraid or uncertain

    Pray the Our Father slowly. When you say “Thy will be done,” think of the situation you want to place in God’s hands.

Catechism of the Catholic Church, nos. 302–307
