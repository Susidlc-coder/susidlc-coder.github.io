About the track

Anoche is a reinterpretation of “Que Soñaba en el Piano” from the album SDLC_02. It combines different genres, styles, and harmonies, all moving toward one goal: making the song fun to listen to.

Scene or visual suggestion

A vast, quiet room filled with soft light. A woman kneels in the center, facing the Tabernacle, while another version of herself speaks to her and tries to pull her away. She remains still and focused. The scene then shifts into ordinary daily life, where she sits at her piano as news, conversations, and human experiences continue to reach her. She takes everything in and tries to understand it, firmly repeating, “No, no,” because nothing will separate her from Him.

About the track

The Rebellion moves through two competing flows, with the piano and drums constantly colliding to create a restless, confrontational sound. About the track

My teacher challenged me to create a song with a more “solid beat”. For some reason, my brain interpreted that as, “Do they want me to make something predictable, like reggaeton? Ugh.” So I did. I created a reggaeton song in my own style. It is Susi’s one and only reggaeton song, built around a single lyric: “What are you gonna do, Mr. Dog?” Irreverent and playful.

About the track

Disdain is about an ugly feeling that surfaced one day when I experienced genuine envy toward someone. I examined the emotion and realized how much it felt like poison spreading through my heart and entire body. I wrote the song from the perspective of rejecting that feeling, addressing it directly and declaring that I never want to experience it again.

About the track

Te Quiero is a song I wrote after being ill for some time in a foreign country. It is about moving through a tunnel and finally seeing a distant light. Even the smallest glimpse brings hope, along with the realization that the only way to reach it is by learning true self love.

Mood and atmosphere

Atmospheric, Cinematic, Dreamlike, Warm, Reflective, Peaceful

Genre and musical style

New Age / meditative, Cinematic / soundtrack, Ambient / atmospheric, Contemporary instrumental, Singer songwriter

Instrumentation and sound

Piano, voice, bass, drumpad

Scene or visual suggestion

A winged figure sits inside a dark cave, her wings three times the size of her body. Mud covers the feathers, hiding their natural pearl white color. Deep within the cave, she discovers an old grand piano. She does not know what it is or how to use it, but she sits down and gently places one finger on a key. A sweet sound fills the cave and immediately captures her. She begins pressing more keys, discovering endless combinations of notes capable of expressing every part of her life. The melodies are not created so she will be remembered. They are necessary for her survival. Everything she has carried inside can finally leave her through the music. The sounds feel as though they must be released and shared rather than kept within her.

About the track

This is a traditional Catholic Christmas carol from Spain, I believe. My husband Francisco and I were in DrummerLab’s practice studio. As he played, I began singing the carol and suddenly decided to record it and add harmonies. I consider it a bonus track because it preserves the feeling of a rehearsal. At the end, I intentionally left in a conversation about editing the song and its pitch. I chose not to clean it up because that unpolished moment became part of the song’s character.
